1 · THE PROBLEM

Viewers of Game LIVE, are passive consumers.

Streamers get an ace, steal a dragon, or make the final circle kill—viewers can only watch, then send a chat message. For those 5 seconds when the highlight happens, viewer attention is at its peak, but they have no action to "participate" with. Gifts are one-way, chat is text-only, and they leave after watching. The viewer's emotional peak during the highlight isn't captured, and the chance to convert them into followers and return viewers is wasted.

~3.5s
Average viewer interaction delay when a highlight happens (from typing chat to submitting)
30s
Encore gives viewers a "participation window"—play a mini-game based on the highlight right away

2 · WHAT IS ENCORE

Reconstruct the streamer's highlight moment in real-time into a 30-second mini-game viewers can play.
👁

OBSERVE

AI Vision continuously watches the LIVE feed, identifying highlights like kills / clutches / aces / dragon steals. Essentially automating the "streamer presses highlight button" action.

🧬

STRUCTURE

Extract the highlight into a JSON config: game type, map theme, enemy count, starting HP, weapon, scene description. This is the V2G "language".

🎮

RECONSTRUCT

The client renders the config into a voxel-pixel mini-game within 30 seconds, popping it up as a bottom sheet to the viewers present at that time.

4 · THE 3 TEMPLATES (PHASE 1)

Not real generative, but template-parameterized.

Phase 1 (Path B) core decision: AI does not write game code, only produces JSON config. 3 pre-built templates cover the three main content categories of TikTok Gaming LIVE. Coverage ~60-70%.

FPS · Tactical Shooter

  • Right-drag to aim · Recoil curve
  • 12-round mag + fast reload
  • Cover tactics + Peek & headshot
  • 1v3 clutch pacing

Core UX Iron Rules

Encore mini-game slides up as a bottom sheet covering 80% of the screen. The LIVE video keeps playing, the streamer's audio keeps sounding, just pushed to the background layer.

  • Never pause LIVE, never mute, never compress main view
  • Player can swipe down to return to LIVE at any time, game auto-ends
  • Auto-ends after 30 seconds, bounces back to LIVE, non-intrusive
  • Runs on $100 phones: Canvas 2D + voxel blocks, no WebGL
  • UX state machine 8 states (see Lark §11.7) — SAMPLING / DETECTED / WARMUP / PLAY / END / REPLAY_PEEK / GIFT / RESET

6 · GIFT ECONOMY

4-tier gift hierarchy, playable without gifting.

Iron rule: Gifting to play is forbidden. Gifting amplifies the experience, it is not a ticket. Streamer share ≥ regular LIVE gift share.

ENHANCE
Modify your next round
"Fire bullet" skin, "Double HP" start, "Extra life"… Personal buffs.
SPOTLIGHT
Expose your high-score clip
Increase chance your clip enters the streamer's Top 3 review pool. Social-oriented.
SPONSOR
Buff the streamer's next round
Give the streamer an enhanced Encore / theme for their next LIVE highlight. Gift flow goes to the streamer.
LOOT
Randomized lootbox skin
Open cases for rare gear skins, sent to personal collection. Recurring revenue.

9 · LOCKED PRODUCT DECISIONS

Design truth lives in Lark, code follows docs.
PATH
Path B (template parameterization), no real generative. AI produces JSON config → routed to 3 templates.
ART
Abstract pixel style (Roblox / MC). Avoids all game IP risk, compatible with $100 phones.
ENTRY POINT
LIVE-first. No short-video feed entry point before M12 (conversion collapses).
UX RULE
Bottom sheet slides up over, never compresses LIVE main view. 8-state finite state machine.
MONETIZATION
4-tier gift economy (Enhance / Spotlight / Sponsor / Loot). Gifting to play is forbidden.
TEMPLATE COVERAGE
3 templates required (FPS + MOBA + BR), covering ~60-70% of Gaming LIVE content.
CREATOR SHARE
Streamer share of Encore gifts ≥ regular LIVE gift share. Ecosystem binding.
DEMO SCOPE
Client + LAN deployment. No backend services (observer.py is a local proxy, not backend).
